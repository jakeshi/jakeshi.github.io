date: 2024-08-02

People keep asking me why I'm getting back into writing and building after years of mostly staying quiet. The honest answer: becoming a parent changes how you think about time.

Before my kid, I could afford to wait. "I'll build that someday." "I'll write about it eventually." Now I feel the days compressing. Not in a stressful way — in a clarifying way. If I have an idea, I should try it. If I learned something, I should write it down. Not for an audience, but because the act of putting it out there makes me take it more seriously.

The other reason is AI. We're in the middle of something genuinely new. LLMs are changing how people interact with information, and I want to be building in this space, not just watching from the sidelines. I have ideas — some half-baked, some I think could actually help people — and this blog is where I'll work through them.

So: why now? Because I have less time than ever, and that's exactly why I can't afford to keep waiting.
